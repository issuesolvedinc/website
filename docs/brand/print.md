# Print

## Business card

3.5 by 2 inches, decided by the founder 2026-11-05.

- **Front**, on white: the mark at the left of the wordmark, the same height. Below it the headline in the site style, 13 pt: "Transform your Business with AI, / Reclaim your Time." On the right, a navy QR code to `https://issuesolved.ca` with `issuesolved.ca` under it.
- **Back**: the dark mark centred on midnight navy `#0A1F44`.

QR code: error correction M, 25 by 25 modules, navy on white, decoded and checked to read `https://issuesolved.ca`. Scan a test print before a full run.

Preview page: `docs/design/business-card.html`.

## Print colour **(proposed)**

The values here are screen values (sRGB). For offset printing, ask the printer to match from sRGB, or use these starting points and approve a proof:

| Colour | sRGB | CMYK starting point |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight navy | `#0A1F44` | 100 85 35 45 |
| Emerald | `#0F7B5F` | 88 25 70 15 |
| Orange | `#FF9A00` | 0 45 100 0 |

## Other print pieces

Letterhead, invoices and proposals are not designed yet. When they are: white ground, the lockup once at the top, DM Sans, the brand colours only for the mark and at most one accent per page. Invoices are signed with the founder's real name.
